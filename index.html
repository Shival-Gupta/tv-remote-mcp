Quick Setup

How It Works

Get up and running in under two minutes with three simple steps.

1

Open the remote on your phone

Open the web remote on your mobile browser while connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your TV.

Launch remote web app →
2

Start the local relay on your PC

Run the lightweight Python relay on your PC. It translates browser commands into native TV control protocols.

bash
pip install pywebostv requests python server/relay.py
3

Control your TV

Select your TV brand, enter your TV's local IP address, and take full control of power, volume, apps, and navigation.
